# Inner Products, Norms &amp; Geometry

Deck 04 of the [Linear Algebra for AI / ML](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/LLM_Hub_Linear_Algebra) series.

**Live presentation:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/Linear_Algebra_AI_04_Inner_Products_and_Norms/

Length, angle and orthogonality &mdash; the geometric language behind cosine similarity, attention scores, the $1/\sqrt{d}$ scale in scaled dot-product attention, and why random vectors in high dimension are almost orthogonal. Includes an interactive cosine-similarity explorer.

## What's inside

- The dot product three ways (algebraic, matrix, geometric)
- Norms &mdash; $L^1$, $L^2$, $L^\infty$, Frobenius, spectral
- Cauchy-Schwarz with two-line proof; triangle inequality as a corollary
- Cosine similarity, the relation to Euclidean distance on the unit sphere
- Orthogonality, orthonormal bases, the $Q^\top Q = I$ property
- Mahalanobis inner product, Gram matrix, the kernel trick in two sentences
- High-dimensional concentration: random vectors are almost orthogonal
- Why scaled dot-product attention divides by $\sqrt{d}$ (Vaswani et al. 2017)
- Interactive cosine-similarity explorer (drag $\mathbf{u}$, $\mathbf{v}$; watch dot, cosine, distance)
